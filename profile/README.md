# Aye

We build **AyeFace**: consent-based identity, loyalty and payments for commerce, in store and online.
A person who chooses to enrol is verified at any participating checkout, their rewards resolve on their own,
and the payment routes to the right rail, in one interaction.

**One platform, many surfaces.** A native checkout app for merchant devices, a web checkout for phones and
online stores, a user portal, a merchant portal, a partner API, and an MCP interface for AI assistants and
AI Agents.

**Built for many markets.** Multiple acquirers and national QR schemes, multi-currency pricing, and
data-protection alignment across GDPR and the APAC frameworks.
